📞 Call Flow

1. Opening

Start Naturally & Find Interest

“Hey, is this [Customer Name]?”

Pause and wait for them to confirm before continuing.

“Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Company]. I was just wondering if there’s anything inside or outside your home you’d like to get a free estimate on. Maybe the roof, kitchen, painting, bathrooms, flooring, or any renovations or updates you’ve been thinking about?”

Use only services your company offers. Give the homeowner time to answer.

2. Project Discovery

Ask About Their Specific Project

“Got it! Tell me a little more about what you’re hoping to change.”

Choose the questions that match their project—not all of them.

Flooring: “About how big is the area you’re looking to redo? It doesn’t have to be exact, just a rough idea.”

Roofing: “Are you thinking of replacing the whole roof, or just repairing part of it?”

Plumbing: “Are you looking to replace all the kitchen plumbing, or is there a specific issue you’d like fixed?”

Kitchen: “Roughly how big is the kitchen, and what would you want to change—cabinets, counters, flooring, or the whole layout?”

“And what would be the most important part of the project for you?”

3. Full Scope

Uncover Other Needs

“That makes sense. And besides that, if you could change one more thing about your home right now, what would it be?”

“Is there anything else you’d want the specialist to look at while they’re there?”

If they have no additional work, move on without pushing.

4. Homeowner, Decision-Maker & Timeline

Confirm Ownership & Timing

“Just to confirm, are you the homeowner and the person who makes the decisions about improvements at [Full Property Address]?”

“If the estimate looks good and everything works for you, would you be open to starting the project within the next couple of months?”

“We may have some promotions available right now. If the price and timing make sense, would starting within 60 days be realistic for you?”

Pick one timeline question. Mention promotions only when verified. If multiple owners decide together, ask whether they should both be present.

5. Conditional Financial Readiness

Discuss Payment Comfortably

“And if you like the estimate and decide to move forward, how would you prefer to handle payment? Would you pay out of pocket, or would you be interested in financing options, if available?”

If financing is requested and the company uses a 620 threshold: “To help the specialist explain which financing options might be available, do you happen to know if your credit score is around 620 or higher? It’s okay if you’d rather discuss that directly with the financing team.”

Ask about credit only when necessary for an actual financing program; never promise approval or request sensitive account information.

6. Firm Appointment

Offer a Confirmed Slot

“Great. I have [Available Day and Date] at [Available Time]. Would you be able to make that appointment?”

“Perfect, I’ll put you down for [Day, Date, Time] for a free, no-obligation estimate on [Project].”

Offer only real available slots. Do not mark an appointment as booked until it is actually scheduled.

7. Confirm Details & Obtain Consent

Verify, Recap & Close

“Before I let you go, can I double-check your full name and the property address?”

“And is this the best phone number to reach you on?”

“So we’re looking at [Project Summary] at [Address], and your appointment is [Day, Date, Time]. Is that all correct?”

“Do you agree to this appointment, and is it okay for our team to contact you about confirming it?”

“Perfect, [Name]. Thanks so much for your time. We look forward to meeting you. Have a great day!”

Explain the actual confirmation process, if any. Respect a refusal or request not to be called.
